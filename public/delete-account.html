Delete your Tesella account

Tesella · for players on the web and the Android app

In the game (quickest)

  1. Open Tesella (the app, or tesella.io) and sign in.
  2. Tap Account on the main menu.
  3. Tap Delete account, confirm with your password (or, if you use Google sign-in, type your username), and tap Delete forever.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Can't sign in?

Email support@tesella.io from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account" and your username. We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: username, display name, email address and password (or Google sign-in link).
  • Your ratings, rank, stats, match history and Marathon scores, and your saved Marathon game.
  • Your list of blocked players, and everything that keeps you signed in.

In other players' match lists, your name is replaced with "deleted player". Reports other players have made about you may be kept until they've been dealt with. Our host's recovery copies of the database expire within 30 days.

Settings and replays saved on your own device stay there until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
