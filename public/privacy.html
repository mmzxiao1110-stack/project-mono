Privacy Policy

Tesella · Last updated: November 26, 2026

Tesella is a game you play on the web (tesella.io) and in our Android app, made by Tesella ("we"). This page explains what we collect when you play, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: support@tesella.io.

If you only play offline

Games against the AI, pass-and-play games and Marathon played without an account run entirely on your device. We don't receive anything about them. Your settings (theme, volume, controls) and your replays are saved on your own device.

If you play as a guest

Online without an account, you choose a guest name. It is shown to the players you meet and kept only while you're connected. Guests have no record and nothing is saved about them.

If you make an account

To play ranked games and keep your progress we store:

  • Username and display name. Shown to other players with your rating and rank.
  • Email address. Used only to send verification and password-reset codes, and to confirm a request about your account. We don't send marketing email and never show your email to other players.
  • Password, stored only as a salted scrypt hash. We can't read it. If you sign in with Google instead, we receive your email address and a Google account ID from Google, and no password.
  • Game record: rating, wins and losses, time played, your recent ranked matches (opponent's name and the score), and your Marathon scores. A Marathon game you play signed in is sent to us (its starting seed and your moves) so we can check the score and let you resume it later.
  • Players you block. Kept with your account so their chat stays hidden and you're never matched with them.
  • A sign-in token saved on your device so you stay logged in, valid for 30 days.

What other people can see

Your player profile is public on T-Terminal (tesella.io/terminal) and in the game's profile popups: your display name, rating and rank, wins and losses, time played, your rating over your last 40 ranked games, your recent matches, and your best Marathon score. Your email address and how you sign in are never shown. Accounts that haven't verified their email aren't listed.

Chat, reports and blocking

During online matches you can chat with your opponent. Messages pass through our server to the two players in that match and are filtered for blocked words and limited in length and rate. We keep the last few lines only in memory while the match lasts and don't store chat.

If you report a player, we store the report (who sent it, who it's about, the reason, any note you add, and the recent chat between you two) and email it to our moderators so we can act on it. Reports are kept until they've been dealt with and are no longer needed. The person you report isn't told who reported them.

If you block a player, their chat no longer reaches you and matchmaking never pairs you with them.

Technical data

Our server sees your IP address while you're connected. We use it in memory to limit abuse (for example, too many sign-ups from one network) and don't store it with your account. Our host keeps server logs for a limited time; they record usernames and match events, and an IP address when an abuse limit is triggered.

Who else handles it

  • Cloudflare hosts the game, its server and its data.
  • Resend delivers our emails: it receives your email address and code when we send you one, and the contents of reports we email to our moderators.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your account, email, sign-ins, ratings, stats, Marathon scores and blocks straight away; in other players' match lists your name is replaced with "deleted player". Our host keeps recovery copies of the database for up to 30 days, so deleted data disappears from those within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Delete your account any time in the game: Account, then Delete account. Or see tesella.io/delete-account.
  • Change your display name in Settings.
  • For a copy of your data, or if you can't sign in to delete your account, email support@tesella.io from the address on your account.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights over your data. You can contact us, or your local data protection authority, about them.

Children

Tesella isn't directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will be noted in the game.
