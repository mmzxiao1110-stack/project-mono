If you only play offline

Solo and take-turns games run entirely in your browser. We don't receive anything about them. Your settings (theme, volume, your block deck) are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device.

If you make an account

To play ranked games online we store:

  • Username and display name. Your display name, rating and rank are shown to the players you're matched with.
  • Email address. Used only to send verification and password-reset codes. We don't send marketing email and don't show your email to other players.
  • Password, stored only as a salted scrypt hash. We can't read it.
  • Game record: rating, wins and losses, time played, and your recent matches (opponent's name and the score).
  • A sign-in token saved in your browser so you stay logged in, valid for 30 days.

What other people can see

Your player profile is public on T-Terminal (tesella.io/terminal) and in the game's profile popups: your display name, rating and rank, wins and losses, time played, your rating over your last 40 ranked games and your recent matches (opponent's name, score and when). Your best Marathon score (and its lines) is also public. Your email address and anything about how you sign in are never shown. Accounts that haven't verified their email aren't listed.

Chat

During online matches you can chat with your opponent. Messages go through our server only to be passed on to the two players in that match. We don't store them, and they're gone when the match ends. Messages are filtered for blocked words and limited in length and rate.

Technical data

Our server sees your IP address while you're connected. We use it in memory to limit abuse (for example, too many sign-ups from one network) and don't store it in your account. The server's logs, which our host keeps for a limited time, record usernames and match events, and an IP address when one of those abuse limits is triggered.

Who else handles it

  • Render hosts the game server and its data ([REGION, e.g. United States]).
  • Resend delivers the emails with your codes, so it receives your email address and the code.

We don't sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept until you ask us to delete it. Backups of the game database are kept for up to 48 hours, so deleted data disappears from them within two days.

Your choices

You can change your display name in Settings. To get a copy of your data or have your account deleted, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address on your account. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights over your data, and you can contact us or your local data protection authority about them.

Children

Tesella isn't directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has made an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will be noted in the game.
